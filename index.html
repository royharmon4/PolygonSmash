How to play

Move your pointer inside the play area to aim. Click or tap to fire. On mobile, drag to aim and lift your finger to shoot.

  • Matching shapes merge only when they make calm, controlled contact.
  • Gravity pulls upward — the pile grows down from the ceiling.
  • The top ceiling is fixed; danger is now the lower warning line.
  • If any piece stays below the danger line for about 2 seconds, it's game over.
  • Two capstones (tier 10) explode and clear nearby pieces.

Prototype notes

Circle physics under the hood, polygon visuals on top — keeps it stable and easy to expand.

Good next upgrades: sound, particles, charge shots, and power-ups.
