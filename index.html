How to play

Aim in the arena, then click/tap to fire. On mobile, drag to aim and lift to shoot.

  • Inverted gravity pulls pieces upward, so stacks hang from the fixed ceiling.
  • The tier ladder is fixed: matching equal tiers makes the next tier.
  • The lower danger line is fixed. If any settled piece stays below it for ~2 seconds, the run ends.
  • Dodecagon capstones explode when merged and clear nearby pieces.

Arcade notes

Use the Next 2 queue to plan safe placements before the danger timer starts.
