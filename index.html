How to play

Drag or move to aim. Release to fire. Keyboard: Space fires, P pauses, R restarts, and ←/→ adjust aim.

  • Matching shapes that touch will merge into the next tier.
  • Gravity pulls upward, so the stack grows downward from the ceiling.
  • Do not let the pile sit below the red line for too long.
  • Fast chain merges build a combo multiplier.
