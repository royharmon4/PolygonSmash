How to play

Aim, then click/tap to fire. On mobile, drag to aim and lift to shoot.

  • Gravity is inverted: pieces pull upward and settle against the fixed ceiling.
  • The ladder is fixed: match two equal tiers to create the next tier.
  • The danger line is fixed near the bottom. If a settled piece stays below it for about 2 seconds, you lose.
  • Dodecagon capstones explode on merge and clear nearby pieces.

Arcade notes

Plan with the Next 2 queue to keep a clean merge path before the danger timer starts.

Fast planning beats panic shots.
